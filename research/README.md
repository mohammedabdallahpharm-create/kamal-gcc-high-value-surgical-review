# Research Workstreams

Create one dated research brief per workstream. Each brief should state its question, scope, sources inspected, findings, counter-evidence, unknowns, and next actions.

## Planned workstreams

- `regulatory-licensing/` — legal perimeter, licensing, clinical responsibility, data governance
- `insurer-tpa-operations/` — authorization, claims review, escalation, case management, outsourcing
- `surgical-market/` — procedure mix, high-cost case evidence, cost definitions, provider activity
- `second-opinion-landscape/` — existing services, providers, delivery models, commercial positioning
- `procurement-commercial-signals/` — tenders, contracts, partnerships, expansion, supplier signals

Do not mark a workstream complete until its source coverage and evidence gaps are documented.
