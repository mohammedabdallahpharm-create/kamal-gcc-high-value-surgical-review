# KAMAL GCC
## Independent Medical Second Opinion Network
### High-Value Surgical Review Observatory

Kamal GCC is a research and development initiative exploring a structured connection between **independent medical specialists** and **health insurers / third-party administrators (TPAs)** for complex, high-value surgical cases.

The Observatory examines the regulatory, clinical, operational, data-governance and commercial conditions required for such a model to operate responsibly across the UAE and, subject to further research, the wider GCC.

> **Current status:** Research and model validation. This repository does not represent an operating clinical service, a licensed healthcare facility, a licensed TPA, or a regulator-approved business model.

## Concept under examination

A potential coordination platform may help insurers and TPAs identify and engage appropriately qualified independent specialists for case-specific medical second opinions.

- **Insurer / TPA:** commissions or requests a review and retains its own contractual and coverage responsibilities.
- **Kamal GCC (proposed coordination layer):** may coordinate case intake, specialist matching, documentation, communication and workflow, subject to regulatory classification.
- **Independent specialist:** reviews clinical material and provides an independent professional opinion within their licence and scope.
- **Treating physician:** retains the clinical responsibilities assigned under applicable law and professional standards.

The actual service boundary, data access, contracting structure and licensing requirements remain under investigation. The name or description of a service does not determine its legal classification; actual activities do.

## Initial research scope

- **Initial geography:** United Arab Emirates.
- **Potential expansion lens:** GCC, only after jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction research.
- **Initial use case:** complex / high-value surgical cases.
- **Indicative case-value threshold:** AED 500,000 as a research hypothesis only; not a legal, regulatory or verified market threshold.
- **Potential customers / counterparties:** health insurers and TPAs.
- **Potential supply side:** appropriately licensed independent specialists and, where required, licensed healthcare facilities.

## Research workstreams

1. Regulatory perimeter and licensing
2. Specialist credentialing, independence and conflicts of interest
3. Insurer / TPA operating models and procurement
4. Clinical review workflow and decision rights
5. Health-data governance, hosting and security
6. Market landscape and existing alternatives
7. Commercial model, contracting and liability
8. GCC jurisdiction mapping
