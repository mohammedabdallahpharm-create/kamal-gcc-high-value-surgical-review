# Sources

This directory contains source inventory records and, where useful, source-specific research notes.

## Priority source families

1. UAE federal and emirate-level regulators and legislation portals
2. Official health-insurance and claims data portals
3. Insurer and TPA disclosures, policy documents, provider manuals, and annual reports
4. Hospital and healthcare-group annual reports and service disclosures
5. Government procurement and tender portals
6. Clinical guidelines, peer-reviewed studies, and medical societies
7. Credible independent market research and journalism

## Source handling

- Prefer the original publisher's URL.
- Record access date and coverage period.
- Mark snippets separately from inspected sources.
- Save relevant extracts with enough context to avoid changing their meaning.
- Record inaccessible sources rather than silently dropping them.

The master source register is maintained in `../data/source-register.csv`.
